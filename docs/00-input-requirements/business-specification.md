# Business specification

For P1 the business requirements are the [Hiwar UI Specification](Hiwar-UI-Specification.md) together with the design boards in `Hiwar-claude-code-handoff.zip`, refined into the [feature specs](../01-product-requirements/specs/README.md). The feature specs state every decided behaviour, including the product decisions taken after the UI specification (pricing, billing unit, trial, operator portal). The product specification the UI specification cites is not part of the inputs.
